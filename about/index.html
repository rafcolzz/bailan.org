About

In Spanish, bailan means “they dance” or “you all dance,” coming from the verb bailar (to dance).

In Chinese internet slang, bǎilàn (摆烂) means “let it rot” or actively giving up on a hopeless situation.

Spanish Meaning (Bailan)

  • Translation: “They dance” (ellos/ellas bailan) or “you formal plural dance” (ustedes bailan).
  • Tense: Present indicative form of the regular verb bailar (to dance).
  • Usage: Ellos bailan salsa (They dance salsa).

Chinese Slang (Bai Lan / 摆烂)

  • Literal translation: “Set it out to rot” or “let things rot”.
  • Meaning: Deliberately giving up or letting a bad situation get worse because fixing it feels impossible.
  • Origin: Started in NBA basketball slang for losing on purpose to get a better draft pick, later used by youth to describe burnout and coping with high social pressure.
